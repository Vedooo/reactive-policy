reactive-policy {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

The operator is now watching ReactivePolicy resources across the cluster.

1. Check the operator is running:

   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "reactive-policy.fullname" . }}

2. Apply a sample policy:

   kubectl apply -f https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Vedooo/reactive-policy/main/config/samples/api_error_rate_guard.yaml

3. Inspect it with the rp CLI:

   rp policy list -A
   rp action audit --since 1h
   rp plugin list

{{- if .Values.webhook.enabled }}

Admission webhooks are enabled. Policies are validated at admission, and
approval decisions are stamped with the approver's identity from the
authenticated request.
{{- if .Values.webhook.certManager.enabled }}
The serving certificate is issued by cert-manager; if the operator pod is not
ready, check that cert-manager is installed and the Certificate was issued:

   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get certificate {{ include "reactive-policy.fullname" . }}-webhook
{{- end }}
{{- else }}

NOTE: the admission webhooks are disabled (webhook.enabled=false). Invalid
policies are caught at reconcile time rather than rejected at admission, and
approval gates hold the pipeline but cannot prove who approved: the approver's
identity is taken from the object instead of the authenticated request, and
decisions are not write-once. Enable them with webhook.enabled=true.
{{- end }}
